# School Transport System

School Transport System is a elegant web application that is meant to manage school transport services of any learning institution. It has a wide variety of features such as:

- Vehicle monitoring using geo fence, that is meant to track bus movements in its desingnated geo fence. If vehicle moves outside of its geo fence then it send alerts to appropriate staff for further investigations.
- Mobile app for parents to track vehicles assigned to their children.
- Notifications are sent to a parents mobile app when vehicle is about to pick up and drop child.
- Payment of transportation fees through mpesa and credit/debit card.
